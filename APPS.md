## Voiceinator

Speak it. It lands clean.

Menu-bar dictation for Mac. Nothing leaves the machine. Free. Apple Silicon M1 or newer, macOS 14 or newer.

Site: https://voiceinator.com/

Download: https://updates.boringlabs.online/Voiceinator-1.0.0.dmg

From the terminal:

```bash
curl -fsSL https://voiceinator.com/install.sh | bash
```
