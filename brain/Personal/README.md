# Personal

Your own life admin: home, family logistics, travel, appointments, the practical layer.

The slower identity material — beliefs, values, what you're becoming — goes in `Life/`.
Private notes about *other people* go in `People/`, behind the firewall.

`Personal/inbox/` is the drop zone.
