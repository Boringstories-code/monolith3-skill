# Meetings

One note per meeting or call.

The note that earns its keep has three parts: what was said, what it actually meant, and
what you now owe someone. Raw transcripts do **not** belong here — they go to
`00-OS/raw/work/` and get ingested. A transcript pasted into a meeting note is a wall nobody
reads, including the agent.

Action items graduate out of here into `Projects/<slug>/` or `Inbox/`.
