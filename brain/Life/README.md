# Life

The identity layer: what you believe, what you want, what you keep relearning.

Not tasks and not admin — those are `Personal/`. This is the slower material: values,
convictions, recurring patterns, the lines you'd want said back to you.

`Life/inbox/` is where raw captures land (voice memos, late-night notes). The distill step
turns them into one card per belief, deduplicated — one belief, one card, evidence attached.

This folder shapes how the agent talks to you. It never becomes content in outward-facing
work.
